What We Collect

When you book a trip or ask for a package quote by phone, WhatsApp, or through this website, we collect the information needed to respond — typically your name, phone number, pickup and drop locations, travel dates, and group size.

How We Use It

  • To confirm a booking or quote request and coordinate with a driver
  • To contact you about a trip, delay, or change
  • To follow up on a quote you asked for

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information is shared only with the assigned driver as needed to complete the trip.

Data Retention

Booking and enquiry records are kept for a reasonable period for accounting and follow-up purposes, then deleted or anonymized.
